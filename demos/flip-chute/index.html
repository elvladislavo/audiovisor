Pose keys

The jumper is spheres and capsules with flat toon shading, on 11 joints. Each joint's rotation comes from named poses: run, crouch, tuck, arch, pull, hang, flare, land. These are blended with smoothstep between key times.

Flip

The body group spins 2.5 turns' worth of π about its centre of mass, 0 → 5π/2. That is one full front flip, then a quarter turn more to end belly-down.

Physics

Height and speed are integrated at 240 Hz with drag. The terminal velocity drops from 26 m/s to 5 m/s as the canopy opens, then to 1.8 m/s in the flare.

Canopy

A 24-gore dome with a crumpled morph target. It inflates from 1 to 0 crumple. The 24 lines are rebuilt every frame from the rim to the jumper's shoulders.
